Family-run, for the neighborhood

Every morning our family opens the doors on Evergreen Village Square to pour your coffee, make your sandwich and say good morning like we mean it.

Made fresh, made to order

Breakfast and lunch sandwiches are made when you order them, on the bread you pick: bagel, croissant, wheat, white or sourdough.

Pastries worth the stop

Big muffins, banana bread, coffee cake, flaky croissants and danishes, ready in the case next to your morning coffee.

Coffee your way

Espresso drinks, house drip, chai, cold brew, frapps and fresh-blended smoothies, with sugar-free syrups and soy or almond milk whenever you ask.

Our front porch is the town square

The library next door, the park and its gazebo, the shops around the plaza, and our tables in the shade of the arcade.

Weekday mornings

Grab a fresh sandwich and a coffee on your way to work, or slow down with a latte after school drop-off. We open at 6:30.

Wednesdays and Sundays

The Evergreen Farmers Market sets up in the Village Square from 9 am to 1 pm. Grab a latte and a bagel and walk the stalls.

After school

The Village Square library branch is a few steps away. A Kids Hot Chocolate is 3.50, and homework goes faster with a muffin.

All year round

Summer brings free Friday-evening concerts at Music in the Square, and family events like the kids’ Halloween celebration keep the plaza lively.

Room to stay

Tables inside and under the arcade, free Wi-Fi and no rush. People work, study or meet friends here for the whole afternoon.

Big orders welcome

Coffee and sandwiches for the office, the team or the whole family? Order at the counter. Our crew handles large orders and special requests.

Made your way

Sugar-free syrups, soy or almond milk, an extra shot, a breakfast plate with no bread, a kid-size hot chocolate. Just ask.
